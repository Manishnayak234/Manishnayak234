## Manish Nayak

**ADAS Perception Engineer** · Radar & camera perception · Edge AI deployment

I build perception features for advanced rider and driver assistance systems, working from sensor data to warning logic that holds up on the road.
I work on radar-based Forward Collision Warning for two-wheelers, and I deploy vision models on edge hardware.
M.Tech in Mechatronics from IIT Patna, with a patent on adaptive radar profiling.

### Focus Areas

- **Radar perception**: target filtering by ROI, SNR and RCS; Time-to-Collision (TTC) warning logic for Forward Collision Warning on two-wheelers
- **Camera–radar calibration**: using camera ground truth to calibrate radar detection and warning thresholds
- **Edge AI deployment**: model quantization and inference on the Horizon RDK X5 BPU, with an IMX219 camera over MIPI CSI
- **Functional safety**: working exposure to ISO 26262 and SOTIF (ISO 21448) in ADAS development
